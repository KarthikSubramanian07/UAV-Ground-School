Notes:

* Checked on 2026-10-08. PX4 v1.17.0 (May 2026) is the latest stable; its Cube Orange+ build enables the same drivers as v1.16.2 for every row here, still without crsf_rc or message signing.
* Betaflight 2025.12 has no CAN, no MAVLink missions and no waypoint mode; 2026.6 adds all three in early form, and the waypoint autopilot is only compiled with the USE_FLIGHT_PLAN build option.
* Every Betaflight cell describes what the firmware can do. On this exact drone none of it works until someone writes a Cube Orange+ board config (IMUs, timers, ADC pins), and the 8 MAIN outputs behind the IOMCU cannot be used at all.
* INAV, Betaflight's sibling with GPS navigation, adds position hold, return to home and waypoint missions; its CAN, Cube and SIYI support was not checked.
* Least certain cells: PX4 with the SIYI A8 mini (no official word on MAVLink gimbal v2), PX4 with a TFmini-S on I2C, PX4 naming the Here4, and Betaflight's brand new DroneCAN and autopilot, which the release notes call initial and simulation only.
