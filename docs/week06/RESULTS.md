Times here and in every table are simulated seconds, PX4's clock, counted from when the flight recorder started (the launch starts PX4 at the same moment). PX4 SITL runs in lockstep with Gazebo, and the 8 GB M1 ran the simulation at down to half real time during the busiest flights, so wall clock durations would overstate every rise and settling time; `week06.flight` maps the recorder's clock onto PX4's through the timestamps of `vehicle_local_position`.
