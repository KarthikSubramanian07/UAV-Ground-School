Privacy

Privacy on UAV Ground School.

UAV Ground School is a static educational showcase. The site does not create user accounts, does not sell personal information, and does not run a first party analytics product that identifies individual visitors for advertising.

What this site processes

Pages are hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Like most public websites, the hosting and CDN layer may record standard request metadata such as IP address, user agent, requested URL, referrer, and timestamps for security, reliability, and abuse prevention. Those operational logs are controlled by the host's policies and retention settings, not by a custom application database on this project.

Interactive demos on the showcase (color segmentation, detector explorer, wiring diagram, PID playground) run in your browser against assets already delivered with the page. They do not upload your images to a project operated backend. If you choose to open GitHub links, GitHub's own privacy policy applies to that service.

Cookies and third parties

This site loads webfonts from Google Fonts. Your browser may contact Google's font servers when rendering pages. The project does not set marketing cookies and does not embed third party ad networks. Cloudflare may set cookies or equivalent client signals required for bot management or performance of the edge network according to Cloudflare's documentation.

Source code and contributions

If you open an issue or pull request on the public repository, the content you submit becomes part of the GitHub project history under GitHub's terms. Do not include secrets, private flight logs, or personal data you are not willing to publish. To ask a question without filing an issue, use the channels listed on the contact page.
