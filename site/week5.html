    Talking to ROS 2 without ROS 2.

    week05/participant.py is a DDS participant in pure Python: SPDP and SEDP discovery, reliable RTPS with heartbeats and acknowledgements, DATA_FRAG reassembly, CDR, and ROS 2's naming and type hash conventions. Pointed at the unmodified tutorial nodes, it holds up in every direction.

    Topic, service or action?

    The lecture's quick check. A topic is a stream nobody answers; a service is a quick question with one answer; an action is a long job with progress you can cancel.

    1. Take off and climb to 5 m.

    2. Reset the odometry origin.

    3. Send camera frames to the detector.

    4. Switch the flight controller to position hold.

    5. Land, with the option to abort.

    One slow callback, three executors.

    A 50 ms timer shares a node with a subscription whose callback sleeps 300 ms; a message arrives every 500 ms for 3 s. In one mutually exclusive group the timer waits ({{STAT:w5_block_single}} ms worst gap). In separate groups on a MultiThreadedExecutor it keeps time ({{STAT:w5_block_groups}} ms).
